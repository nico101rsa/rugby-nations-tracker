iPhone, iPad or Mac: tap “subscribe”, then Subscribe.
Android / Google Calendar: copy your team’s address, open calendar.google.com on a computer → Other calendars → + → From URL, and paste it.
Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web, and paste the address.

Subscribe, don’t import — an imported copy never updates. That’s why the addresses above are plain text, not links: tapped on an iPhone, one would import the fixtures once and never update them. Times can move; the app is the live source. The 30-minute alert can be switched off in your calendar app.

Data

The points tables in the app are worked out from match results, never typed in: standings.json. Where a try bonus or a tiebreak is still waiting on information, the table says so rather than guessing.

Rugby Nations Tracker is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to World Rugby or any national union. All match data is provided by third-party sources.
