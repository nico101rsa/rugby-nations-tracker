Privacy Policy — Rugby Nations Tracker

Last updated 4 October 2026

Rugby Nations Tracker has no accounts and no advertising. It never asks for your name, email address or contact details. It does send a small amount of anonymous usage information, described below, so the developer can see which parts of the app people use. None of it is linked to you.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app uses Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count how the app is used. When you do one of these things, the app sends Aptabase a short note naming it:

  • opening the app;
  • switching tab (Fixtures, Results, Standings or your team's tab);
  • following a team, with which team it was;
  • opening a team's page or its fixture list, with which team;
  • being offered a team app icon, and choosing or resetting one, with which team;
  • the app asking iOS to show its rating prompt, with the reason (your team won, or a longer visit), and tapping "Write a review";
  • turning the full-time alert on or off, and opening the app from one.

Each note also carries the app's version number, your device's language setting (for example "en-AU"), a flag that marks the developer's own testing in a web browser (it is always off in the app you install), and a random session number. That number is held only in the app's memory and is replaced after an hour of inactivity, so it cannot follow you from day to day. Your iOS version is read from the standard information every web request carries.

Aptabase uses your IP address when a note arrives to work out an approximate country and region, and to count daily users with a one-way code whose secret changes every day. It does not store your IP address, and the app collects no advertising identifier or device ID. The developer sees only totals in Aptabase's dashboard (for example, how many people opened the Standings tab this week, or how many follow Japan). This information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you across other apps or websites. See Aptabase's privacy policy for how it handles this data.

The teams you follow are stored on your device. Apart from the anonymous notes above, they are not sent anywhere. There is currently no in-app switch to turn analytics off; if you have questions about it, use the contact address below.

Match data

When you use the app, match fixtures, results and standings are fetched from data files we publish on GitHub Pages, which we build from third-party sports data providers. Standard technical information (such as your device's IP address) is processed by the hosting service to deliver these files. We do not receive it, log it or use it to identify you.

Typefaces

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts when it starts. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address and the standard information the request carries (such as your iOS version). The request says nothing about you or how you use the app. See Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

The app can remind you 30 minutes before a team you follow kicks off. It can also tell you when that team's match has finished: the full-time alert is off until you turn it on, and it never includes the score. Both are scheduled and shown entirely on your device, from fixture data it has already downloaded. No server is involved, and no device token is created. Scheduling or showing a reminder or alert sends nothing anywhere. You can switch the full-time alert off again in the app, and turn all of the app's notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

If a future version offers live score notifications, delivering those would require an anonymous device token. It would not be linked to your identity, and this policy will be updated before any such version ships.

Tips

You can leave an optional tip through an in-app purchase. Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer never receives your name, your Apple Account, your payment details or any other personal details; Apple's reports to developers show only totals. A tip unlocks nothing: the app is exactly the same whether or not you leave one. The app does not record tips in its analytics.
