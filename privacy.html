Privacy Policy — Rugby Nations Tracker

Last updated 22 August 2026

Rugby Nations Tracker does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics software.

Match data

When you use the app, match fixtures, results and standings are fetched from our server, which retrieves them from third-party sports data providers. Standard technical information (such as your device's IP address) is processed transiently to deliver this data and is not logged or used to identify you.

Notifications

The app can remind you 30 minutes before a team you follow kicks off. If you allow notifications, these reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device, from fixture data it has already downloaded. No server is involved, no device token is created, and which teams you follow is never sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

If a future version offers live score notifications, delivering those would require an anonymous device token. It would not be linked to your identity, and this policy will be updated before any such version ships.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
