# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When a NEW candidate concerns the national squad (selection, injury, discipline, coaching), lead with it over club results or older items, even if its retrieval score is lower. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Do not lead with a match report more than a few days old or a pre-match opinion piece when a fresh result or news item is on the shortlist. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Lead with the highest-scoring NEW men's item about this nation's own side (result, selection, injury, coach). Pick a club item only if no such item exists, and then say 'club' in the heading and first sentence. Never lead with an event that merely happens in the nation or involves a relative of a player. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Do not skip the top NEW men's item on the grounds that it is 'already covered' unless the same story is already in this edition. State the news directly instead of describing what 'the report' or 'the coverage' says. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- When the pack holds a men's national-side item (result, selection, injury return), lead with it over a women's, age-grade or pathway story; if a women's item does lead, write 'women's' in both the heading and the first sentence. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Lead with the national side itself (result, selection, injury, coach or captain news) over a club match report or a story that only mentions a national-team player or location; if you must run a club item, put the national-team hook in the heading. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- When the shortlist holds a coach's reaction, hire or selection story on the men's national side, prefer it to individual player ratings or family-interest pieces. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- When the pack has a NEW item on the men's national side (result, coach reaction, selection), lead with it; use a club, sevens or family item only if no such item exists. _(added 2026-10-18)_
