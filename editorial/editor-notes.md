# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When the team has just played, lead with its own match result or injury news from a NEW item over side events (legends/Classic XV fixtures, pre-match press quotes, other teams' angles). _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Before leading, scan every NEW item for your own team: prefer an official team announcement, selection or injury news, or formal union/competition business over a profile or colour feature on one player. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Put the team's own reported fact (who is out, who is picked, what happened) in the heading; keep pundit or rival-team comment in the body, not the heading. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Spend the 55-90 words on the lead event's own details; drop old-history asides (last year's quotes, previous results, a player's 'reputation') unless the item itself makes them the point. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Give each player's full name on first mention in the body (e.g. 'Sam Prendergast', not 'Prendergast'), and identify coaches by role, unless the heading already supplies the full name. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- When a team has just been named, lead with the selection's headline change (star return, captain, debutant, key omission), not a bench or secondary angle. _(added 2026-11-06)_
- Every sentence must be reported fact; do not add connecting inferences about what a quote or selection 'shows' or 'fits'. _(added 2026-11-06)_
- Before writing, check that every figure and claim in the paragraph agrees with the others (a count in the heading matches the names listed; a 'winless' record doesn't sit beside a recent win); cut any figure that conflicts. _(added 2026-11-05)_
