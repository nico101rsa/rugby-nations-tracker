# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure the heading identifies a specific person or organization involved in the story; avoid generic references like 'Japan Rugby League One faces legal action'. _(added 2026-07-31)_
- When news is thin, prioritise identifying a key individual involved in the situation to ground the lead paragraph. _(added 2026-07-31)_
- Ensure all headings follow the 'Who + Did What' format, avoiding vague statements that lack subject-specific action. _(added 2026-07-30)_
- Eliminate 'filler' sentences that frame tournament context (e.g., 'remains the 34-19 win' or 'searching for solutions') in favour of tighter, fact-heavy prose. _(added 2026-07-30)_
- Ensure kicker labels are unique and descriptive; avoid overusing generic headers like 'Tournament Structure' when specific topical labels (e.g., 'Logistics', 'Scheduling') are available. _(added 2026-07-29)_
- For Ladder storyline editions, ensure the prose synthesizes the search results into a specific team context rather than reciting the general tournament debate broadly. _(added 2026-07-29)_
- Ensure headings provide specific news (what happened) rather than vague descriptive summaries of departmental updates. _(added 2026-07-28)_
