# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer a men's national-team item (selection, availability, coaching, result) over a club or women's item. If you must lead with a club story, make the heading name the player and state the national-team link. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Make the kicker describe the story's real subject. Do not use national-team kickers like 'Squad boost' on club news, and never use an unnamed descriptor like 'France star' in a heading. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Lead only with a story whose subject is this team's men's side; if the item is about another nation or the women's game and merely mentions this team, pick a different candidate even if it scores higher. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Make the heading carry the biggest fact in the body (e.g. the returning players, not the routine squad announcement), and never let the heading's description of a player contradict the body. State only details found in the chosen item. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Before choosing, check the highest-scoring NEW item. Lead with it unless it was clearly already run; never swap it for a lower-scored anecdote or a club-level update with no named players. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- When a squad, injury, contract or result item exists, lead with that team fact. Use pundit commentary only when nothing harder is on offer, and then headline what happened to the team, not what the pundit said. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Never mention 'coverage', 'reports', or when something was previously run; state only what happened, who said it and when, and write nothing the shortlist doesn't support. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Make the heading's claim match the body exactly (who is out, for how long, ruled out versus rested); if the source says 'considerable time' or is vague, use its wording rather than a precise figure. _(added 2026-10-21)_
