# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure headings always contain a specific action verb and outcome (e.g., 'Ruled out', 'Handed start', 'Returns to') rather than generic 'Prepare for' or 'Look ahead' phrasing. _(added 2026-09-27)_
- Strictly maintain the default 'men's' labelling policy: headlines about women's matches must explicitly include the 'women's' label to avoid conflation with the men's international circuit. _(added 2026-09-27)_
- Ensure all headings include a concrete verb and the full context of the team/event, avoiding passive or atmospheric phrasing. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Maintain strict adherence to the 90-word limit; some bodies are approaching the upper threshold and should remain concise. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Ensure all headlines follow a consistent format: [Subject] [Action/Status] [Specific Context]. Avoid using 'as' clauses or semicolons in headings. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- When reporting on player transfers or injury news involving domestic clubs, ensure the specific club competition (e.g., URC, Premiership) is mentioned to provide necessary context for the reader. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Ensure every heading includes the team's gender or level (e.g., Women, U20, Sevens) if they are not the primary senior men's national side. _(added 2026-09-23)_
- Maintain strict neutrality in headings; avoid descriptive modifiers like 'resilient' or 'massive' to keep the tone objective. _(added 2026-09-23)_
