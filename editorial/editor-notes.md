# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure the kicker consistently distinguishes between genders or age-grade teams when the subject matter deviates from the men's international default. _(added 2026-10-07)_
- Maintain a clear distinction in the body text: ensure the first sentence explicitly defines the scope (e.g., 'The Red Roses' or 'the Scotland women's squad') to prevent ambiguity. _(added 2026-10-07)_
- Ensure every heading explicitly identifies the team or player in a full, active sentence. _(added 2026-10-06)_
- When covering non-standard formats (e.g., League, Women's, Sevens), ensure the primary label is included in the very first sentence of the body text. _(added 2026-10-06)_
- Ensure the first sentence of all 'data' or 'storyline' editions includes the specific team name to avoid ambiguity. _(added 2026-10-05)_
- When reporting on coaching searches or movements, lead with the specific entity the coach is joining or leaving to ensure the headline is immediately legible. _(added 2026-10-05)_
- Ensure headings for 'Storyline' or 'News' editions focus on the highest-impact narrative available; prioritize national team management or World Cup context over individual club-level performance when both are on the shortlist. _(added 2026-10-05)_
- Maintain the established register for 'Data' editions: avoid narrative framing and focus strictly on statistical trends. _(added 2026-10-05)_
