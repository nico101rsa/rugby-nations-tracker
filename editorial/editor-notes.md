# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure all kickers are distinct and topical; avoid generic labels like 'Transfer news' or 'Selection call' when more descriptive phrasing is possible. _(added 2026-07-25)_
- Maintain high-precision in headings by ensuring the primary subject (player or coach) is explicitly named and their specific action is defined. _(added 2026-07-25)_
- Standardize kicker casing to Title Case across all editions; currently, multiple editions (Argentina, South Africa, and the Data rungs) are using ALL CAPS inconsistently. _(added 2026-07-25)_
- Ensure every heading includes the full name of the primary subject (player or coach) to avoid vague references like 'Japanese bolter'. _(added 2026-07-23)_
- Vary sentence structure in opening lines to avoid over-reliance on 'The fallout from [event] has intensified' or similar templates. _(added 2026-07-23)_
- Ensure the kicker is strictly tied to the team's specific news, avoiding generic categories like 'Tournament honours' if a more specific topical angle exists. _(added 2026-07-22)_
- Maintain high-precision attribution in the body; when citing a coach or pundit's opinion, ensure the distinction between their claim and the team's factual reality remains clear. _(added 2026-07-22)_
- Never fuse two independent statistics into a single phrase. Each number carries its own unit and denominator, and a record is not a quantity of points: write "unbeaten in three, 130 points scored", never "an unbeaten record of 130 points scored".
