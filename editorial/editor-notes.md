# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure kicker casing is consistent across all editions (Title case, e.g., 'Squad selection' or 'Tragic loss'), avoiding all-caps. _(added 2026-08-21)_
- In Storyline editions where no fresh news is found, avoid phrases like 'There is no coverage in today's press.' Instead, synthesize the current status of the thread as the lead fact. _(added 2026-08-21)_
- Always lead with the highest-salience news item (e.g., major injuries or deaths) even if it is non-competitive; do not bypass it for routine transfers or tactical analysis. _(added 2026-08-20)_
- Do not include names, specific financial figures, or coaching assignments unless they are explicitly contained within the provided retrieval shortlist. _(added 2026-08-20)_
- Ensure the kicker is thematically aligned with the body's primary focus; do not use 'Coaching' as a kicker if the content is fundamentally a player-performance or match-review breakdown. _(added 2026-08-19)_
- When summarizing match results in the body, prioritize objective outcomes (scorelines, standings) before integrating quotes or tactical analysis. _(added 2026-08-19)_
- Avoid 'meta-commentary' in the body text (e.g., 'With today's press coverage offering few details' or 'according to latest coverage'). Focus exclusively on the facts of the story itself. _(added 2026-08-18)_
- Ensure all headings include the specific team name or primary subject involved to improve scannability. _(added 2026-08-16)_
