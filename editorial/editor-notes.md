# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When a heading cites a score, make the winner unmistakable ("Ireland beat Springboks 20-17"); avoid constructions like "X's victims" or "after a Springbok win". _(added 2026-11-24)_
- Write each stat sentence so it says who did what against whom; drop any figure you cannot phrase plainly, and reconcile counts such as changes or tries with the pack. _(added 2026-11-24)_
- Once app data shows a fixture is played, give the final score in the first sentence (only as app data supplies it), build the lead on the outcome, and put team sheets, previews, permutations and announcements in past tense as background; never present the result as pending, and when the lead is a follow-up angle (injury, quote) put the scoreline in the heading beside the named person. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- When the lead concerns a development, second-string or age-grade side (e.g. Ireland XV, Fiji Latui, U20s), say so in the heading and first sentence, and never imply it is the senior national team. _(added 2026-11-21)_
- If the top story is excluded as already run, take the next NEW story about the senior side's coming Test (selection, captain, injury, opponent) before age-grade results, broadcast or tournament-logistics items. _(added 2026-11-21)_
