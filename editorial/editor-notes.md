# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure every edition, including the opening sentence of the body, explicitly identifies the team as the 'women's', 'U20', or 'sevens' side if they are not the primary men's international team. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Maintain strict adherence to the roundup constraint: headlines must be 12 words or fewer, use telegraphic headline style, and include the gender identifier if non-men's. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Always include the team qualifier ('women', 'U20', or 'Sevens') directly in the heading when covering non-men's international rugby. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Ensure headings follow the 'someone does something' active structure rather than leaning on dependent introductory clauses. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Prioritize match results and major tournament outcomes over secondary context (like player milestones or recruitment administrative history) when both are available in the shortlist. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Ensure the kicker reflects the primary focus of the heading; if the heading is about a match win, the kicker should reflect the competition/tournament standing. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- Ensure the leading story is the most newsworthy event available in the shortlist, prioritizing match results and significant disciplinary rulings over speculative coaching rumors. _(added 2026-09-21)_
- When crafting the 10-12 word heading, ensure it clearly identifies the primary subject and the specific outcome rather than focusing solely on the 'role' or 'position'. _(added 2026-09-21)_
