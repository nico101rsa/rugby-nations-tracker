# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- On first mention in the body, give every person's full name and role (e.g. 'Wales contact skills coach Dan Lydiate', 'New Zealand head coach Dave Rennie'); never open with a bare surname or 'the head coach'. _(added 2026-11-11)_
- End the paragraph on a fact about this team; do not close on another team's news (e.g. an opponent's injury). _(added 2026-11-11)_
- Keep headings and sentences to one clean subject-verb chain; do not stack appositives or two subjects before one predicate (e.g. 'Fiji's Saturday opponents Ireland lose…', 'Montoya failed an HIA and Oviedo, both in doubt…'). Rewrite until it parses on first read. _(added 2026-11-10)_
- When the team played since the last edition, lead with the result or its decisive moment rather than a side detail (late switch, club transfer, minor knock) unless that detail is itself a selection bombshell. _(added 2026-11-09)_
- Never gesture at a reaction or detail the source doesn't spell out ('was blunt', 'of note', 'two players'). Give the actual words or names, or leave the claim out. Name a person in every heading. _(added 2026-11-09)_
- When the team has just played, lead with its own match result or injury news from a NEW item over side events (legends/Classic XV fixtures, pre-match press quotes, other teams' angles). _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Before leading, scan every NEW item for your own team: prefer an official team announcement, selection or injury news, or formal union/competition business over a profile or colour feature on one player. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Put the team's own reported fact (who is out, who is picked, what happened) in the heading; keep pundit or rival-team comment in the body, not the heading. _(added 2026-11-07)_
