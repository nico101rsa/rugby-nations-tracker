# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritize concrete match developments or squad news over speculative punditry/podcasts in the lead section. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- Ensure headings strictly follow the 'Subject + Active Verb + Significant Event' structure; avoid vague descriptors like 'continues discussions'. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- Ensure the heading summarizes the most significant outcome of the match or event, prioritizing match results over player retrospectives or speculative interviews. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- When a player or coach is quoted in the heading, ensure the action (e.g., 'warns', 'backs', 'explains') is specific and avoids generic verbs. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- Ensure match reports are reconciled across team editions; if one team records a heavy defeat, it must be acknowledged in the corresponding team's briefing. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- Tighten headings to focus strictly on the immediate outcome rather than speculative future intent. _(added 2026-09-20)_
- Ensure the 'heading' strictly adheres to the 10-12 word limit; several headers are currently pushing toward 15+ words. _(added 2026-09-19)_
- Prioritize active, declarative verbs in the opening of the 'body' paragraph to maximize the impact of the kicker's premise. _(added 2026-09-19)_
