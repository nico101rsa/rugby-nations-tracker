# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Lead with a men's national-team story (squad, selection, injury, coach, result) whenever the shortlist holds one, including NEW items you haven't run; use women's, age-grade or club items only if no men's story exists. _(added 2026-10-23)_
- Lead only with a story about this team itself; if the item is mainly another team's player or coach (an opposing captain's quotes, a rival coach's job talks), pick the team's own squad, selection or result news instead. _(added 2026-10-23)_
- Lead with a story about the current men's national squad or its players; never lead with a listicle, a retired player's off-field story or a club-fixture note when a Test, squad or selection item exists in the shortlist. _(added 2026-10-23)_
- Before writing, confirm that your heading's central claim comes from a news item in the shortlist, not from a list or 'seven players who…' piece, and state it only as that item reports it. _(added 2026-10-23)_
- Quote record, standings and latest-result figures only as the app data gives them; never add match counts yourself or mix in other competitions. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- If the body names a venue or city, it must match the edition's match field; otherwise omit the location. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- Introduce every quoted or named person with a role or club on first mention (e.g. 'Saracens' Brendan Venter'); never use a bare surname in a quote. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- Lead with the highest-scoring NEW item about the team's own men's national side (squad, fixture, injury, coach) before club colour, podcasts or pundit opinion; never claim a NEW item was 'already run' unless the pack shows it. _(added 2026-10-21)_
