# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritize analytical or tactical news over speculative opinion pieces or 'rating' articles when building the lead. _(added 2026-09-19)_
- Ensure headings provide specific, concrete action (e.g., coach appointments or results) rather than relying on abstract, inspirational metaphors. _(added 2026-09-19)_
- Ensure the 'heading' strictly adheres to the 10-12 word limit; several headers are currently pushing toward 15+ words. _(added 2026-09-19)_
- Prioritize active, declarative verbs in the opening of the 'body' paragraph to maximize the impact of the kicker's premise. _(added 2026-09-19)_
- Ensure headings clearly specify the individual's role and the specific action taken; avoid vague terminology. _(added 2026-09-18)_
- When reporting on recent match results that appear in multiple team briefs, align the narrative tone to ensure consistency across the suite. _(added 2026-09-18)_
- Ensure the cited 'source' reflects the publication providing the core commentary, not just the aggregator outlet where the content was retrieved. _(added 2026-09-18)_
- When summarizing team news, focus on the action (e.g., 'recalls') rather than the intent (e.g., 'targeting a recall') to keep headings punchy and active. _(added 2026-09-18)_
