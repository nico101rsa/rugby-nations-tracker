# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When a NEW item concerns the national squad, selection or an injury to the team's own players, lead with it over club-competition results or features on former players. _(added 2026-11-02)_
- When leading from a club match or pundit column, headline what actually happened (result, scorer, or the pundit's argument). Name the club in the heading and first sentence. Do not frame it as national selection news unless the source says so. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Prefer a shortlist item with concrete facts (score, named players, decision) over one that is only a headline. Prefer the post-match result over a pre-match item on the same fixture. Never write sentences describing what the coverage lacks. _(added 2026-11-01)_
