# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritize active international match results and serious disciplinary or social issues (e.g., racism allegations) over off-season human interest stories or cross-sport charity events. _(added 2026-08-03)_
- Verify the current season status before describing a story as 'off-season' news; do not lead with charity stories when the team is actively competing in a tournament like the Nations Championship. _(added 2026-08-03)_
- Ensure the kicker reflects the primary focus of the paragraph; for cross-team stories, prioritize the perspective of the nation being briefed. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Refine headings to ensure they are active and concrete, avoiding passive phrasing even in data-heavy or ladder-briefed editions. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Avoid repeating the primary subject of the heading within the first five words of the body; prioritize moving directly into the consequence or context of the event. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Ensure the kicker is the most prominent thematic label for the story; avoid generic labels like 'Match Review' if a more specific context (e.g., 'Regional Reform' or 'Squad Selection') is available. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Ensure headings are punchier and more action-oriented; avoid phrasing like 'vow to remain' or 'gives verdict on', opting instead for direct, active verbs that state the outcome or core news event. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Ensure kickers remain purely descriptive or thematic (e.g., 'REGIONAL REFORM') rather than emotive or editorializing (e.g., 'LEADERSHIP BLOW'). _(added 2026-08-01)_
