# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure the cited 'source' reflects the publication providing the core commentary, not just the aggregator outlet where the content was retrieved. _(added 2026-09-18)_
- When summarizing team news, focus on the action (e.g., 'recalls') rather than the intent (e.g., 'targeting a recall') to keep headings punchy and active. _(added 2026-09-18)_
- Ensure headings always contain a specific person's name and a concrete action; avoid vague descriptions like 'counsel' or 'silence'. _(added 2026-09-17)_
- When a Ladder edition lacks fresh search results, focus strictly on the structural context of the open thread rather than lamenting the lack of press coverage. _(added 2026-09-17)_
- Maintain consistent sentence-case formatting for all kickers (e.g., 'Injury blow' rather than 'INJURY BLOW'). _(added 2026-09-16)_
- Ensure consistency when reporting on multi-lateral tournament topics (e.g., Six Nations US hosting) to avoid conflicting narratives between team editions. _(added 2026-09-16)_
- Maintain strict word count discipline; ensure that the paragraph does not exceed 90 words while providing sufficient context. _(added 2026-09-16)_
- Ensure the body text clearly attributes inflammatory or highly subjective claims to the source cited in the heading, rather than presenting them as objective analysis. _(added 2026-09-15)_
