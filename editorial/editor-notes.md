# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Do not invent or assume coaching identities or player statistics; the New Zealand edition incorrectly named Dave Rennie as coach and fabricated Test start records for Ruben Love. _(added 2026-09-12)_
- Ensure internal consistency across the suite; the Italy edition contradicted the Ireland edition regarding the Italian union's official stance on US-based fixtures. _(added 2026-09-12)_
- When assigned a STORYLINE edition, you must lead with the specific subject thread provided (e.g., ball size, selection plys) rather than reverting to the general shortlist. _(added 2026-09-12)_
- Avoid meta-commentary in the body text regarding the lack of news coverage (e.g., 'there is no coverage in today's press'); simply provide the data or storyline update as a matter of fact. _(added 2026-09-12)_
- Ensure headlines remain focused on the team's status or event, avoiding phrasing that highlights the absence of news (e.g., 'remains unchanged' vs 'no fresh news reported'). _(added 2026-09-11)_
- Maintain the professional, objective tone of the 'Data' editions; avoid unnecessary filler that describes the lack of incoming press. _(added 2026-09-11)_
- Strictly adhere to the 'men's international rugby' scope; if retrieval is dominated by Women's XV (WXV) or other women's internationals, disregard them in favor of men's team news or fallbacks. _(added 2026-09-11)_
- Ensure headings always specify the nature of the event when reporting on mixed-gender news cycles to avoid confusing the reader. _(added 2026-09-11)_
