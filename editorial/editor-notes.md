# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure the heading explicitly names the primary subject and a specific action to improve immediate clarity. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Maintain strict adherence to the Ladder brief: in storyline editions, prioritize the status of the ongoing thread over fresh match-report data found in the shortlist. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Ensure headings always focus on the primary subject of the edition, avoiding focus on external teams if a more relevant team-specific development is available. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Tighten the prose in the body paragraph to ensure the 55-90 word limit is utilized for impact rather than repetitive context. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Ensure 'Storyline' editions strictly prioritize the assigned backlog subject over current injury/news updates when the press retrieval is dry. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Maintain consistent attribution for match reports; ensure the 'source' object accurately reflects the primary news origin. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Ensure cross-edition consistency: if multiple nations are reporting on the same tournament-wide development, synchronize the 'status' (e.g., confirmed, rejected, or ongoing). _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Maintain uniform detail levels in team lists; if including a full starting XV, the bench should be treated with either equal detail or omitted for brevity. _(added 2026-09-13)_
