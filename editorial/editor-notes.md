# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Never mention 'search', 'searches' or 'headlines' in the copy; state what is known and the date it was reported, e.g. 'No return date has been reported since 25 September'. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- End on the last concrete fact; drop closing filler such as 'plenty to ponder', 'must plan around' or 'far from the pitch', and make every heading name a person or team and what happened. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- End the paragraph on a reported fact or the next fixture; never close with an unsourced forecast, a selector's mood, or a remark about what the coverage lacks. _(added 2026-10-11)_
- Check every number and date against the pack before writing, and say 'next Saturday' or 'last Saturday' rather than a bare weekday where two matches could be meant. _(added 2026-10-11)_
- If the lead event is more than a week old, say so in the first sentence (e.g. 'In April') and keep the heading from reading as breaking news; use the correct tense. _(added 2026-10-11)_
- Mark club and league stories in the heading (e.g. 'URC') and the first sentence, but never narrate the label ('a club match rather than a Test', 'a club-level story'). _(added 2026-10-11)_
- Ensure headings for non-senior international stories (Women’s, U20, or Sevens) explicitly include the team category to prevent confusion. _(added 2026-10-11)_
