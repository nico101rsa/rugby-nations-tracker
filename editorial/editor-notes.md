# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure headings prioritize active, subject-driven verbs; avoid starting with 'The ongoing...' or 'The...'. _(added 2026-09-07)_
- Maintain strict narrative consistency across international match-ups; cross-reference the series state with the opposing team's edition to ensure dates and match counts align. _(added 2026-09-07)_
- Ensure headings for match-result editions explicitly include the final score to provide immediate context. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- When summarizing 'ladder' data, avoid repetitive phrasing like 'In the same 11-match window' to improve flow. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Ensure headings follow a strict 'Subject-Action-Result' structure (10-12 words) to avoid passive or vague phrasing. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- When reporting on matches involving two teams covered in the suite, verify the location and context across both briefs to ensure factual alignment. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Ensure kicker categories are concise and consistent; avoid overly descriptive phrases like 'Selection Ambition' or 'Coaching suspension' when a more standard 'Team News' or 'Discipline' suffices. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- When an open-thread 'storyline' search returns multiple results, synthesize the findings to present a cohesive update rather than listing them sequentially. _(added 2026-09-06)_
