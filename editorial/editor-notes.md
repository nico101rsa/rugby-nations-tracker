# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritize on-field competitive results and squad selection news over celebrity or 'crossover' entertainment stories (e.g., TV show castings), especially during active tournament windows like the Nations Championship. _(added 2026-08-11)_
- When a match result in the retrieval contradicts results in other team packs, prioritize current squad news or injury updates over the suspect match report to avoid internal contradictions. _(added 2026-08-11)_
- Ensure the kicker provides more descriptive variety; avoid using 'Transfer news' for every player movement if the context involves contract impasses or complex departures. _(added 2026-08-09)_
- When drafting headers for 'Storyline' editions, prioritize the current status of the negotiation or move (e.g., 'confirmed', 'stalled', 'ongoing') rather than simply stating the topic. _(added 2026-08-09)_
- Ensure coverage of multi-team events (e.g., England vs Argentina disciplinary rulings) is cross-referenced for consistency to avoid conflicting narratives. _(added 2026-08-09)_
- Prioritize significant match results (e.g., test match defeats) over 'prospect watch' items when both are present in the shortlist. _(added 2026-08-09)_
- Ensure all headings remain within the 10-12 word range while prioritizing active, punchy verbs. _(added 2026-08-08)_
- Avoid passive phrasing in headlines; focus on the primary subject and their specific action. _(added 2026-08-08)_
