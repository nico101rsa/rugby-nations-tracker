# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Name the principal in the heading and the first sentence; never write 'a former star' unnamed or pad with a recap of an earlier story. _(added 2026-10-14)_
- Lead with a story about this team's own players, coaches or fixtures; a story about another nation's appointment qualifies only if the body is about this team's stake. _(added 2026-10-14)_
- Before choosing, check the NEW flag: if a NEW item concerns this team's selection, injury or result, lead with it over background features, history pieces or evergreen content. Never claim nothing else is fresh without checking. _(added 2026-10-13)_
- Make the team itself the subject of the story. Prefer its players, coaches, selection or injuries over outside pundits discussing an opponent or a club tour. Always name the person in the heading, never 'the captain'. _(added 2026-10-13)_
- Give every person a full name and a role on first mention (e.g. 'Jake White, former Springboks coach'); never use a bare surname such as White, Pietersen, Donnelly or Aramburú. _(added 2026-10-13)_
- Do not append next-fixture or log-position sentences unless they bear on the lead story; end the paragraph on the story itself. _(added 2026-10-13)_
- Attribute opinions, ratings and columns to their author or outlet in the heading, and never state a cause (e.g. 'cost', 'strengthened') beyond what the source itself says. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- When a heading and body both give timing, make them agree and name the date or day of the source, so older items read as dated rather than as fresh news. _(added 2026-10-12)_
