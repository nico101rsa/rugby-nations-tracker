# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When a shared storyline (e.g., the concussion litigation) affects multiple teams, prioritize the specific national governing body's response or local legal context in the body text to ensure distinctness between editions. _(added 2026-08-31)_
- Ensure the heading explicitly identifies the person involved in transfer or contract stories; avoid vague or purely speculative framing. _(added 2026-08-31)_
- When reporting on 'podcast' or 'pundit' commentary, ensure the body text clearly attributes the sentiment as opinion rather than confirmed business or organizational policy. _(added 2026-08-31)_
- Ensure the kicker reflects the primary subject of the heading; in the France data edition, the kicker 'TRANSFER NEWS' is slightly incongruous with the context of a statistical data-desk note. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Maintain the professional, neutral tone of the data editions; avoid transitioning into subjective reporting in the final sentence of these notes. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Keep headlines strictly within the 10-12 word range; avoid overly long descriptive phrasing that bloats the character count. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Ensure kicker categories remain high-level descriptors (e.g., 'Injury update', 'Selection news') to maintain a consistent professional tone across all editions. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Ensure the kicker consistently reflects the content of the body paragraph; avoid using generic labels like 'Match update' if a more specific topical tag (e.g., 'Injury update' or 'Squad rotation') is available. _(added 2026-08-30)_
