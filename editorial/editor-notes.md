# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Name a player or coach in the heading, not just the team. Avoid openers like 'France release…' or 'Wasteful Wallabies…'. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- Close on a fact about this team. Do not borrow an opposing coach's quote as a closer. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- When the shortlist holds a NEW item on the team's own XV, captaincy or squad for its next Test, lead with it; do not skip it as 'already run' unless it is flagged BACKGROUND. _(added 2026-11-27)_
- Never let a lead be mainly another team's captain or coach quotes: if the only items are the opposition's, use the one that carries this team's own selection or news and make it the subject of the heading. _(added 2026-11-27)_
- Do not join two separate events in a heading with 'as' or 'after' unless the source states the link, and keep the body and its closing fact on the lead team's own story. _(added 2026-11-25)_
- Close on a fact that develops the lead story. Do not append unrelated asides such as fan-poll percentages, ticket sales or a repeat of a minor detail already in the heading. _(added 2026-11-25)_
- When a heading cites a score, make the winner unmistakable ("Ireland beat Springboks 20-17"); avoid constructions like "X's victims" or "after a Springbok win". _(added 2026-11-24)_
- Write each stat sentence so it says who did what against whom; drop any figure you cannot phrase plainly, and reconcile counts such as changes or tries with the pack. _(added 2026-11-24)_
