# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- State only what the cited report says: no inferences about other jobs, fixtures or opponents, and no colour about staff or squads that the pack doesn't contain. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- The source field must name the outlet whose report you rely on. Never mention retrieval artefacts such as 'an undated report' or 'a separate report'; if dates are unclear, say so in plain terms or leave them out. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- In storyline editions, state the latest known status and when it was reported; never mention searches, coverage, 'the storyline' or what has or hasn't broken today. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- In data editions, the heading must state a fact the paragraph actually develops; give figures only, with no commentary on what a team 'needs' or where 'the work sits'. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Lead with a story whose subject is this nation's senior men's side. Do not lead with a piece that only mentions the team, a women's or club fixture, or another nation's anecdote, unless nothing national-men's is on the shortlist. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- When the source is a preview, never narrate the match as played. State kick-off or date, and give a result only if the source reports one. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- In data editions, never write 'in the log'. Name the table plainly or give just wins, losses and points. State each fact once, and don't repeat a run length in heading and body. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- In data editions, don't imply a fixture is imminent ('going into the weekend') unless the app data gives a match date. _(added 2026-10-17)_
