# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure all headings follow a strict [Subject] + [Action] format, avoiding vague abstractions like 'New regime' or 'Tournament form' as kickers. _(added 2026-09-04)_
- Prioritize active verbs in headings to ensure the news content—such as a specific resignation or team announcement—is immediately identifiable. _(added 2026-09-04)_
- Prioritize player-driven conflict or squad drama (e.g., captaincy changes, internal disputes) over generic fixture schedules or 'campaign outlook' pieces. _(added 2026-09-04)_
- Ensure headings provide sufficient narrative context; avoid generic phrasing like 'Injury blow' or 'Scrum scrutiny' if a more descriptive summary of the specific event is possible. _(added 2026-09-03)_
- When updating a storyline, lead with the most recent development found in the backlog search to maintain the 'better edition' objective. _(added 2026-09-03)_
- Verify head coaching roles before attributing squad 'ownership' or primary quotes; do not mistake assistants (e.g., Steve Tandy) or predecessors (e.g., Dave Rennie) for the current head coach. _(added 2026-09-03)_
- Maintain geographical consistency for match venues across paired editions (e.g., do not alternate between 'Soweto' and 'Johannesburg' for the same fixture). _(added 2026-09-03)_
- Ensure all headings follow an 'Actor-Action-Outcome' structure; avoid beginning with 'Campaign' or 'Italy/France' as generic placeholders. _(added 2026-09-02)_
