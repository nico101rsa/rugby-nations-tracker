# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure kicker categories are concise and consistent; avoid overly descriptive phrases like 'Selection Ambition' or 'Coaching suspension' when a more standard 'Team News' or 'Discipline' suffices. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- When an open-thread 'storyline' search returns multiple results, synthesize the findings to present a cohesive update rather than listing them sequentially. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Ensure all location-based claims (venues, host cities) are cross-verified against the match data provided in the app, particularly for international touring fixtures. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Maintain high precision in injury reporting; ensure that specific match outcomes or disciplinary bans are consistent across both teams involved in the same fixture. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Ensure headings clearly define the 'Who, What, Where'. Avoid vague nouns; lead with the specific subject (player or coach name) rather than the act (e.g., 'Les Kiss shuffles...' is superior to 'Selection change: Les Kiss...'). _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Maintain high precision with match series context; ensure that when two teams are covered in a shared series, the match count and context (e.g., 'second Test' vs 'rematch') are aligned. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Ensure headings for match-based news explicitly include both the 'what' (e.g., selection change) and the 'who' (specific team or player) to improve immediate scan-ability. _(added 2026-09-06)_
- Maintain the strict paragraph word count range (55-90 words); avoid overly short sentences that break the narrative flow of the 60-second briefing. _(added 2026-09-06)_
