# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure headings are punchier and more action-oriented; avoid phrasing like 'vow to remain' or 'gives verdict on', opting instead for direct, active verbs that state the outcome or core news event. _(added 2026-08-02)_
- Ensure kickers remain purely descriptive or thematic (e.g., 'REGIONAL REFORM') rather than emotive or editorializing (e.g., 'LEADERSHIP BLOW'). _(added 2026-08-01)_
- For 'DATA EDITION' briefs, strictly limit content to match statistics and ranking data; avoid incorporating breaking legal or political news unless it directly pertains to match-day metrics. _(added 2026-08-01)_
- Prioritize match results and serious disciplinary or social issues (e.g., racism, bans) over charity events or 'human interest' fluff when both are present in the shortlist. _(added 2026-08-01)_
- Ensure the kicker reflects the gravity of the lead story; 'CROSS-SPORT CLASH' is inappropriate when the team is involved in a racism scandal or a Test match review. _(added 2026-08-01)_
- Ensure all headings follow the 'Subject + Active Verb' structure; avoid passive phrasing or vague nouns. _(added 2026-08-01)_
- When detailing squad news, prioritize naming the coach making the decision before citing the returning players to provide clearer agency. _(added 2026-08-01)_
- Ensure the heading identifies a specific person or organization involved in the story; avoid generic references like 'Japan Rugby League One faces legal action'. _(added 2026-07-31)_
