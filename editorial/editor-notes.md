# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When the pack has a NEW item on the men's national side (result, coach reaction, selection), lead with it; use a club, sevens or family item only if no such item exists. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- End the paragraph on a reported fact. Do not add a closing sentence of inference, implication or 'the question now is…' that no source states. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- When the shortlist holds a national-team selection, injury or result item, lead with it ahead of club travel mishaps, individual ratings blurbs or club-match reports. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Only state a time anchor such as 'this week' or 'earlier this week' if the source pack gives that date; otherwise use the date the source itself gives. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- In a storyline edition, lead with what the re-check search returned about the named subject; if it shows the thread has resolved, say so. Never swap in an unrelated shortlist story. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Do not lead with a rankings or permutations piece where your team is only a bystander; prefer a story about the team's own squad, selection, result or staff. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- If a source article previews a fixture, check the app's match data. If the game has been played, write it in the past tense with the real result. Make the attribution in the body match the source field. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- State only what the cited report says: no inferences about other jobs, fixtures or opponents, and no colour about staff or squads that the pack doesn't contain. _(added 2026-10-17)_
