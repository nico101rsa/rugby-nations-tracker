Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure match-specific details, such as stadium or city location, are cross-referenced across team briefings to maintain geographical consistency. _(added 2026-09-14)_
- Maintain the established concise narrative style for storyline editions; focus on the current status of the thread rather than repeating historical context. _(added 2026-09-14)_
- Vary the sentence structure in DATA editions to avoid repeating the timeframe (e.g., '11-game period') in every sentence. _(added 2026-09-14)_
- Ensure the body of news editions avoids repeating the exact phrasing of the kicker or heading. _(added 2026-09-14)_
- Ensure headings for Storyline editions are specific to the thread being updated, avoiding vague phrasing like 'continues talks' when possible. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- When reporting on international series, standardize the framing (e.g., specifying the number of Tests) to prevent contradictory narratives across team editions. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Ensure the heading explicitly names the primary subject and a specific action to improve immediate clarity. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Maintain strict adherence to the Ladder brief: in storyline editions, prioritize the status of the ongoing thread over fresh match-report data found in the shortlist. _(added 2026-09-13)_
