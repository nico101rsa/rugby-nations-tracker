# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Keep headlines strictly within the 10-12 word range; avoid overly long descriptive phrasing that bloats the character count. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Ensure kicker categories remain high-level descriptors (e.g., 'Injury update', 'Selection news') to maintain a consistent professional tone across all editions. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Ensure the kicker consistently reflects the content of the body paragraph; avoid using generic labels like 'Match update' if a more specific topical tag (e.g., 'Injury update' or 'Squad rotation') is available. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Maintain the 55-90 word constraint strictly; some paragraphs are leaning toward the lower limit and could benefit from an additional sentence of supporting detail regarding the player impact or wider implications. _(added 2026-08-30)_
- Prioritize team-centric news (squad changes, selection, match performance) over speculative opinion pieces or personality-driven headlines when multiple sources are available. _(added 2026-08-29)_
- Ensure 'Storyline' editions strictly address the specific subject assigned for the backlog search rather than reverting to generic list-style content. _(added 2026-08-29)_
- Ensure factual consistency across editions when covering the same event (e.g., legal rulings) to avoid contradicting dates or details between different team briefings. _(added 2026-08-29)_
- Verify name-role associations against the broader context of the daily data; if a source attributes a known foreign coach to a new team (e.g., Rennie to NZ), cross-reference with other match data in the pack. _(added 2026-08-29)_
