# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Lead with a story about THIS team's own players, coach, squad or fixtures; an opponent's injury or a foreign player leaving a domestic club is not this team's story, so pick the team's own item even if it is BACKGROUND. _(added 2026-10-27)_
- Judge freshness only from the NEW/BACKGROUND flag; never call a BACKGROUND item 'freshest' or 'not yet covered'. _(added 2026-10-27)_
- Lead with a NEW candidate by default; skip one only if the same event is clearly already covered, and never pick a podcast billing, a club fixture or a former player's off-field news over a NEW story about the national side, its squad or its people. _(added 2026-10-26)_
- Before writing, check that every coach, player and score belongs to the team named in the heading; if the source is about another nation's coach or result, write it from the correct team's side or choose a different story. _(added 2026-10-26)_
- Make the heading state something that happened (a signing, injury, selection or result), not what has not yet happened; avoid 'not yet ruled out', 'may need' and 'earn call-ups' before an announcement. _(added 2026-10-26)_
- When the lead is a club match or club contract, name the club in full (e.g. Glasgow Warriors, Dragons) in heading and first sentence, and name which coach is meant. _(added 2026-10-26)_
- When the shortlist holds an item about the team's own men's side (result, ranking, fixture, squad), lead with it rather than an ex-player's legal case, broadcast or business story, or another club's player. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Keep heading and body timing and claims exactly as the source gives them: never recast a played fixture as upcoming, and never turn a hope 'after November' into 'for November'. _(added 2026-10-25)_
