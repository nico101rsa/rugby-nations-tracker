# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- If the team played in the last 24 hours and a NEW match-result report is on offer, lead with the result: score and decisive moment, with the historic or notable fact in the heading. Put pundit quotes, previews and side angles in the body only. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- If the match has already been played, write the heading and body in the past tense and lead with what happened. Never headline pre-match or team-news framing ('to face', 'ahead of') as upcoming. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Reread the finished paragraph for grammar and clarity. Introduce any player by first name and surname on first mention, and rewrite any sentence that does not parse. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Check the edition date and any result in the app data. If a match has been played, write it in past tense, and never frame pre-match comments as if the game is still ahead. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Give every claim in the body a basis in the lead item or app data; do not add coach names, line-up changes or player details the shortlist does not contain. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- The heading must name a specific person or team and the event. If the source doesn't say who (e.g. 'a Grand Slam-winning figure', 'pundits and fans'), pick a different shortlist item or name the person from the pack. _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Give a role or team on first mention of any person who isn't a household name, put every quoted or paraphrased line in clean quote marks, and make the body state the substance of anything the heading quotes (a vow, a claim). _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Translate all non-English source text into English, including quotes and country names in headings and body. Do not leave 'Sudafrica' or untranslated French quotes in the copy. _(added 2026-11-14)_
