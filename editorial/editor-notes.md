# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Once a fixture has been played, never lead with its pre-match news or with scenarios that depend on its result; build the lead on the actual outcome. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- When the lead is a follow-up angle (injury, draw, quote) to a match already played, put the scoreline in the heading alongside the named person. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- If app data shows the match is already played, give the final score in the first sentence and treat any team-sheet, preview or announcement lead as pre-match background in past tense; never write pre-match tense on a played fixture. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- If app data shows a fixture is finished, never present its outcome as pending or conditional; write previews and permutations in plain past tense, and give the final score only if app data supplies it. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- When the lead concerns a development, second-string or age-grade side (e.g. Ireland XV, Fiji Latui, U20s), say so in the heading and first sentence, and never imply it is the senior national team. _(added 2026-11-21)_
- If the top story is excluded as already run, take the next NEW story about the senior side's coming Test (selection, captain, injury, opponent) before age-grade results, broadcast or tournament-logistics items. _(added 2026-11-21)_
- When the top selection story has already run, lead with your own team's next piece of hard news (injury, ban, fine, call-up) rather than a pundit verdict, tribute or the opponent's selection. _(added 2026-11-20)_
- State clubs, positions and records only as the pack gives them: never hedge with 'or', and never add unsourced historical asides. _(added 2026-11-20)_
