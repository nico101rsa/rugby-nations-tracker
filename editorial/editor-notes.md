# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Make the heading claim exactly as strong as the body's evidence (no 'only', 'a doubt' or 'recalling' beyond what the source states). Join facts causally only where the source does, never because they sit in the same paragraph. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Lead with a NEW candidate unless a background item is clearly bigger. Never call a NEW item 'already covered' without evidence in the pack, and never write 'first match in charge' for a coach without checking which team the pack says he coaches. _(added 2026-10-29)_
- Lead only with a story about this team's own squad, coach, results or players. Do not use a club move or another nation's player who merely mentions the team, and never state a conclusion stronger than the source ('ruled out' while a scan is pending). _(added 2026-10-29)_
- When the best pack item is a pundit column or reflective interview (e.g. Moore on Pollock, Townsend on belief), lead with the concrete event instead (fixture, squad, injury, signing) unless the opinion is itself the news. _(added 2026-10-29)_
- Cut interpretive filler the pack does not state ("a sign of...", "good news for..."); every sentence must carry a sourced fact, and the heading must name someone and say what happened. _(added 2026-10-29)_
- When the top story is 'already covered', lead with a genuine follow-up that adds a new fact (a decision, ruling, citation, replacement) and put that fact in the heading; do not switch to a feature, analysis piece or quote that is merely adjacent. _(added 2026-10-28)_
- Make the heading carry the paragraph's most newsworthy fact, and use only claims the body supports (no 'indefinitely' or 'two concussions' if the body says weeks and one knock); do not bury the freshest fact in the last sentences. _(added 2026-10-28)_
- Lead with the highest-scoring NEW item about the team's own men's XV (injury, selection, discipline, coach). Skip it only if the shortlist itself shows a duplicate, and never bury it in the last clause of a different story. _(added 2026-10-27)_
