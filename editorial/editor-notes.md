# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Keep every sentence about the team being briefed: do not pad with another nation's squad news or meta-commentary (e.g. 'a club matter rather than Ireland business'), and introduce any named person on first mention. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- If the lead event is more than a couple of days old, say when it happened in the first sentence and use past tense for fixtures that have already been played; never present an old ruling or appointment as breaking. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- Don't write vague attribution like 'X weighed in' or 'one headline says'; state what was actually said or reported, or drop the line. Don't add background claims (such as a past club or role) that the source doesn't support. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- When the shortlist holds a concrete event (result, injury, selection, appointment, sacking), lead with it over pundit or podcast opinion; if commentary must lead, name the speaker and say it is their view in the first sentence. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- When a men's national-team item exists on the shortlist, prefer it over women's, sevens or club stories even if it is BACKGROUND; do not call an item new or unrun unless it is flagged NEW. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- When a NEW candidate concerns the national squad (selection, injury, discipline, coaching), lead with it over club results or older items, even if its retrieval score is lower. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Do not lead with a match report more than a few days old or a pre-match opinion piece when a fresh result or news item is on the shortlist. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Lead with the highest-scoring NEW men's item about this nation's own side (result, selection, injury, coach). Pick a club item only if no such item exists, and then say 'club' in the heading and first sentence. Never lead with an event that merely happens in the nation or involves a relative of a player. _(added 2026-10-18)_
