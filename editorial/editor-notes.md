# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure the 10-12 word limit for headings is strictly maintained; some current headings are approaching the upper bound and risk losing punch. _(added 2026-08-16)_
- Maintain high precision in attribution; when summarizing a report from a specific source, ensure the tone reflects the original outlet’s framing of the story. _(added 2026-08-16)_
- In Storyline editions, do not use meta-commentary (e.g., 'There is no news today'). If the search provides results, summarize the current status of the thread as requested. _(added 2026-08-15)_
- Ensure headings for ladder editions follow the same journalistic standard as news editions: specific subject, active verb, and outcome. _(added 2026-08-15)_
- When leading with player departures or disciplinary matters, focus primarily on the direct impact to the upcoming matchday squad or tactical setup. _(added 2026-08-15)_
- Verify the names of head coaches against the provided text or known team data; do not hallucinate coaching staff (e.g., attributing the All Blacks to Dave Rennie). _(added 2026-08-12)_
- Ensure every non-ladder edition includes the 'source' object with proper attribution to the lead outlet. _(added 2026-08-12)_
- Prioritize on-field competitive results and squad selection news over celebrity or 'crossover' entertainment stories (e.g., TV show castings), especially during active tournament windows like the Nations Championship. _(added 2026-08-11)_
