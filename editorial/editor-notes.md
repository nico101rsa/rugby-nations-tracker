# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Every non-men's senior international team (women, age-grade, sevens) must explicitly name its category in both the heading and the opening sentence. _(added 2026-10-04)_
- When evaluating shortlists, always prioritize substantive team news, disciplinary bans, and squad selections over generic pre-match hype or opposition warnings. _(added 2026-10-04)_
- Ensure all entries, including the first sentence of the body, clearly distinguish between senior men's international rugby and other categories (Women's, U18, Club) if not already stated in the heading. _(added 2026-10-04)_
- Maintain the strict 'plain stat-desk' tone for Data editions; avoid descriptive filler words like 'proud record' or 'significant milestone'. _(added 2026-10-04)_
- Ensure all headings provide specific context; avoid vague terms like 'highly rated forward' in favor of naming the individual if the brief allows, or clarifying the region/team name. _(added 2026-10-03)_
- Maintain consistent attribution style when multiple editions rely on the same source material to ensure factual parity across the suite. _(added 2026-10-03)_
- When assigned a DATA edition (stat-desk note), do not lead with items from the retrieval shortlist if they failed the salience bar, even if the data angle feels 'thin'. _(added 2026-10-03)_
- Ensure the kicker for a DATA edition accurately reflects the statistical angle (e.g., 'Head-to-head' or 'Scoring trends') rather than using news-style kickers like 'Transfer news'. _(added 2026-10-03)_
