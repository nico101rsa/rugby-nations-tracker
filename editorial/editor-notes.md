# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure all headings remain within the 10-12 word range while prioritizing active, punchy verbs. _(added 2026-08-08)_
- Avoid passive phrasing in headlines; focus on the primary subject and their specific action. _(added 2026-08-08)_
- Ensure every JSON object includes the 'source' block with the 'name' of the publication referenced. _(added 2026-08-08)_
- In Storyline editions, avoid referencing 'searches' or 'results' in the body text; describe the status of the situation as a reported fact (e.g., 'Negotiations remain stalled' rather than 'Search results show no progress'). _(added 2026-08-08)_
- Ensure headings remain strictly within the 10-12 word limit to maintain uniform aesthetic impact across the suite. _(added 2026-08-07)_
- Avoid using quotation marks for single words (e.g., "strong"); use them only for direct speech or verified terminology to maintain professional tone. _(added 2026-08-07)_
- Ensure the heading explicitly links the subject to the outcome (e.g., 'Player X faces Y-match ban for Z' rather than 'Player X receives ban'). _(added 2026-08-06)_
- When citing internal rankings or data, focus on the immediate competitive consequence rather than historical context unless the record is currently being broken. _(added 2026-08-06)_
