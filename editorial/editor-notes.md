# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure headings follow a consistent 'Actor-Action-Outcome' structure; avoid vague phrasing like 'faces fitness wait' in favor of specific details available in the source text. _(added 2026-09-29)_
- When multiple high-impact stories are present, prioritize the one with the highest verified retrieval score to ensure the most significant news reaches the reader first. _(added 2026-09-29)_
- Prioritise national team news over club rugby for the lead section if international results are available, even if the club match features international players. _(added 2026-09-28)_
- Ensure every header follows the structure [Entity] [Verb] [Outcome] to maintain a punchy, news-desk register. _(added 2026-09-28)_
- Ensure all headings for women's, age-grade, or club teams explicitly include the team category (e.g., 'England Women' or 'Zebre Parma') to prevent reader confusion. _(added 2026-09-27)_
- Maintain a professional, journalistic tone in headings; avoid filler words and ensure the primary subject and event are stated concisely within the 12-word limit. _(added 2026-09-27)_
- Ensure the first sentence of every edition explicitly defines the scope (e.g., 'England Women' or 'The Black Ferns') to avoid ambiguity, as the default assumption is men's international rugby. _(added 2026-09-27)_
- Maintain the 10-12 word limit for headings; some today were slightly long or carried excessive internal punctuation. _(added 2026-09-27)_
