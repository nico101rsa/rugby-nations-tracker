# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure headings for narrative stories focus on the consequence of the event rather than just the actor's emotional reaction. _(added 2026-10-03)_
- When drafting data editions, ensure the transition between the historical ranking/form context and the specific tournament/match statistics feels logical and less list-like. _(added 2026-10-03)_
- Ensure any edition covering a women's, age-grade, or sevens team explicitly states this in both the heading and the opening sentence. _(added 2026-10-02)_
- Headings must strictly name the key individual or team and state the definitive action or event without relying on vague summaries. _(added 2026-10-02)_
- Ensure that any edition featuring non-men's international rugby (Women's, Sevens, U20) includes a clear gender/format label within the headline, not just the body text. _(added 2026-09-30)_
- Prioritize action-based headings that clearly state the impact or outcome (e.g., 'Club reform proposed' vs 'Individual endorses proposal') to improve headline readability. _(added 2026-09-30)_
- Ensure headings follow a consistent 'Actor-Action-Outcome' structure; avoid vague phrasing like 'faces fitness wait' in favor of specific details available in the source text. _(added 2026-09-29)_
- When multiple high-impact stories are present, prioritize the one with the highest verified retrieval score to ensure the most significant news reaches the reader first. _(added 2026-09-29)_
