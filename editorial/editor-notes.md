Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure cross-edition consistency: if multiple nations are reporting on the same tournament-wide development, synchronize the 'status' (e.g., confirmed, rejected, or ongoing). _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Maintain uniform detail levels in team lists; if including a full starting XV, the bench should be treated with either equal detail or omitted for brevity. _(added 2026-09-13)_
- Ensure all headings remain strictly within the 10-12 word limit to maintain header uniformity across the suite. _(added 2026-09-13)_

- Ensure total factual consistency across team briefings regarding shared match venues or tournament-wide strategic announcements (e.g., US-based Six Nations fixtures). _(added 2026-09-12)_
- Maintain strict paragraph length adherence; some paragraphs in the ladder editions approach the upper word limit, risking brevity. _(added 2026-09-12)_
