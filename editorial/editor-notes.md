# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure total consistency in cross-team reporting; if a story impacts two teams, verify the tournament context matches in both editions. _(added 2026-10-10)_
- Maintain strict professional attribution in the body copy for all reports derived from institutional documents (e.g., the Keystone report). _(added 2026-10-10)_
- Prioritize major institutional or structural news (such as World Cup hosting bids or head coach appointments) over match post-mortems, refereeing complaints, or marketing/merchandise announcements. _(added 2026-10-09)_
- Pay closer attention to salience scores when multiple storylines are present; do not lead with a low-scoring (e.g., 0.2) match report if a high-scoring (e.g., 50+) major news event is available. _(added 2026-10-09)_
- Ensure all headings include the specific competition or level if the story concerns club, sevens, or age-grade rugby. _(added 2026-10-09)_
- Prioritize international Bledisloe Cup or Championship match news over club-level URC team updates when both are present in the retrieval. _(added 2026-10-09)_
- Ensure all headings follow a strict 'Name + Action' structure: avoid passive descriptions and focus on the primary individual or team moving the news forward. _(added 2026-10-09)_
- When reporting on domestic team selections in international briefings, consistently emphasize the player's international status or relevance to the upcoming Test window to provide necessary context. _(added 2026-10-09)_
