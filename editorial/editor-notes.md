# Standing editor notes

Injected into the writer prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Write headings that state only what the source reports; never add consequence or hope ("hopes dented", "keeping X in place") the story doesn't say. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Do not pad a thin story with unrelated fixtures, stat lines or unnamed 'described as' claims; every sentence must come from the lead item's source or app data and concern its subject. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- When the shortlist holds a national-team item (Test preview, squad, injury to a current international), lead with it rather than a club or URC match report. If you must run a club story, say so in the heading and first sentence. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Cut sentences that comment rather than report ("a sign of how far a side can climb", "this is a club story, not an X one"). Include only ages, rankings and form claims that appear in the shortlist or app data. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- When a men's national-team story is on offer, lead with it over club discipline cases or opinion columns, and name the outlet that actually supplied the facts as the source. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Lead with an item whose subject is this team's own current squad, coach or fixture. A story about a retired player, or about another nation's lessons, is not this team's lead. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- End on a fact from the pack or data. Do not close with speculative filler such as 'will want to', 'would be a statement' or 'a boost for a side'. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Lead with a men's national-team story (squad, selection, injury, coach, result) whenever the shortlist holds one, including NEW items you haven't run; use women's, age-grade or club items only if no men's story exists. _(added 2026-10-23)_
