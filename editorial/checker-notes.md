# Standing checker calibration notes

Injected into the fact-check prompt daily; curated by the post-run tuner.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned, and a note that passes a draft by submission count or "regardless" is discarded. Prune freely.

- Do not reject drafts based on editorial decisions regarding news currency or story positioning if the information is factually accurate. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- Focus exclusively on verifying names, numbers, quotes, and scores against trusted app data; do not evaluate the editorial quality of the content. _(added 2026-10-22)_
