# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritise men's Test results and trophy outcomes (e.g. New Zealand's Bledisloe win) over sevens administration, week-old medals and women's squad announcements. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- Avoid labelling club results with a national prefix ('South Africa's URC Lions'). Include them only when they involve a key injury, a selection bombshell or a Test-level storyline. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- Skip results more than a fortnight old (e.g. France's Round three win over Japan). Choose this week's results, injuries and coach moves, such as Graham ruled out of the autumn Tests. _(added 2026-10-12)_
- Drop hedged lines like 'reported frontrunner' unless the briefing itself confirms the move. _(added 2026-10-12)_
