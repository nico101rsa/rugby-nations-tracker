# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Keep injury headlines to what the briefing states: write 'needs knee surgery', not 'after knee surgery', unless the surgery is reported as done. _(added 2026-10-14)_
- Use the briefing's own verb for availability: 'unavailable' or 'will miss', not 'ruled out', when the player chose to be unavailable. _(added 2026-10-14)_
- Prefer confirmed results, appointments and key injuries (e.g. a coach hired, a big Test scoreline) over unconfirmed coaching rumours. Include at least one men's Test result or appointment whenever the briefings contain one. _(added 2026-10-13)_
- Skip women's-side injuries that were first reported weeks ago, and old news in general. A women's line earns its place only for a result, a trophy or a major selection or injury. _(added 2026-10-13)_
