# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Cut roundup lines only from each briefing's own lead event, never from background or context sentences such as Scotland's Round 3 result, and do not add competition names the briefing lacks. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Skip club-side match reports and ambiguous 'Lions' style club references; prefer national-team results, selection bombshells, key injuries and coaching changes. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Prioritise men's international results (e.g. All Blacks 43-21 Wallabies, 13th straight Bledisloe win) over club results like Bulls v Stade Français. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Use future tense for dated events; Ribbans is eligible from 27 October, so write 'set to become eligible', not 'becomes eligible'. _(added 2026-10-18)_
