# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Cut each roundup line only from the nation's own briefing; never attribute a story to a nation whose briefing does not contain it, as with the England/Fin Smith line. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Prefer senior-men's results, injuries and selection bombshells; include age-grade or routine squad-replacement lines only when nothing bigger is available. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Attribute a line to the team it is about, and copy the briefing's certainty: no 'reportedly' unless the source hedges. The Furlong line is an Irish story filed under Fiji. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Pick selection bombshells and key injuries over marginal omissions or lines cut from pundit speculation. Rennie dropping two backup locks is weaker than Savea and Taukei'aho being out, and the Springboks injury line comes from an opinion piece. _(added 2026-11-01)_
