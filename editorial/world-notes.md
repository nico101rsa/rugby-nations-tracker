# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip single-player women's injuries, especially from stale reports. Prefer men's results, selection bombshells or coach moves, and leave the slot empty rather than filling it with a marginal item. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Do not carry storyline lines where the briefing admits nothing new (e.g. Edwards joining Argentina, reported last week); prefer fresh results, selections or injuries. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Avoid three lines on one match (Bledisloe injuries); consider Wales's Thomas injury, and label club stories such as the Lions lock's death as club. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Write club-level lines unambiguously, for example 'Johannesburg Lions', so they are not confused with the British & Irish Lions. _(added 2026-10-17)_
