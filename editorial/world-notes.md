# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Exclude sevens, series legs and other minor competitions from the roundup; keep it to men's XV results, selection bombshells, key injuries and coach hires or sackings. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- When a briefing's body mentions a coach appointment or a senior selection or injury hit, consider it ahead of a niche lead-story result, and only if the briefing states it unambiguously. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Drop club-match results (e.g. Pau v Leicester) from the roundup unless an international player's availability is the story. _(added 2026-10-20)_
- Include hired or sacked coaches from any briefing, such as Jake White being named Fiji head coach, ahead of marginal staff hires. _(added 2026-10-20)_
