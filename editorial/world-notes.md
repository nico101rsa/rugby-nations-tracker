# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer confirmed men's selection news or key injuries over hedged ('likely') items and minor regional women's or sevens results. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- Do not use a line that restates a hedge from the briefing as fact; skip it if the briefing itself is uncertain. _(added 2026-10-22)_
- Skip evergreen or weeks-old items (e.g. a bid reported on 6 October) and routine squad lists; prefer results, major injuries, coach moves and genuine selection bombshells. _(added 2026-10-21)_
- Exclude off-field legal or honours stories about former players. Pick on-field results, selections, key injuries and coach changes, such as Australia's ranking slide or Fiji's new coach. _(added 2026-10-21)_
