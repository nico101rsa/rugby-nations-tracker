# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip A-team results and training-camp recalls; prefer senior-international injuries, selections and results, e.g. Wales' Dyer/Carter injuries or a key All Blacks injury. _(added 2026-10-26)_
- Prioritise big results and key injuries: Fiji's 34-28 win over Japan and Savea's All Blacks absence outrank Faletau's finger surgery and a minor newcomer. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Do not strengthen a briefing's claim: write 'Faletau out for finger surgery', not 'Wales hope for November return'. For squad lines, name the headline selection, not a debutant. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Exclude club results, injuries to club players and contract or transfer signings (Ulster-Bulls, Page, Leota, van Poortvliet). Pick Test results, national-squad bombshells, key Test injuries or coaching changes. _(added 2026-10-25)_
