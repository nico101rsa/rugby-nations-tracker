# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Do not turn a training-session indication into a confirmed selection ('starts'); keep 'set to' or 'named' wording as in the briefing. _(added 2026-11-18)_
- When a team's briefing leads on an injury or ban, headline the fresh consequence (captain out of Japan game), not the earlier result. _(added 2026-11-18)_
- Fill the available slots, up to four, and prioritise big results and upsets (e.g. Wales beat New Zealand 35-28, France beat South Africa 21-19, Scotland beat Australia 15-12) over a single centre's injury. _(added 2026-11-17)_
- Include coach-controversy stories that fans of other teams would text about, such as Erasmus's match-fixing claim, only if the briefing reports it factually and attributes it. _(added 2026-11-17)_
