# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer fresh, headline-level results and injuries (e.g. England 45-22 Australia, Ntamack's shoulder) over stale results mentioned in passing or minor squad returns such as a prop rejoining. _(added 2026-11-10)_
- Each line must be a single headline of 12 words or fewer, drawn from the briefing's lead event rather than an incidental aside. _(added 2026-11-10)_
- Do not use club transfers of uncapped players (e.g. Camerlinckx to Bath) or union governance/league-structure stories; prefer results, key injuries and selection bombshells. _(added 2026-11-09)_
- Give results and key injuries priority over lower-stakes items: the Springboks' win over Italy, Sheehan's failed HIA and Ntamack's injury were all in the briefings but missing. _(added 2026-11-09)_
