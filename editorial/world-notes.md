# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Only include a line for an event that broke in today's edition; do not recycle background facts such as Jake White's months-old Fiji appointment as news. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Check big stories from the larger nations (e.g. England's player-rift row) before filling slots with lower-stakes lines. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Only use a selection line when it is a genuine surprise or a key absence. Routine confirmations (a captain returning, a fly-half who has started five Tests) are not roundup material. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Do not upgrade a briefing's wording: if the briefing says a berth is 'open to' a player, don't write that he 'starts'. Use 'named' only when the briefing says the side is named. _(added 2026-11-07)_
