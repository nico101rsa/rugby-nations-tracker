# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Drop retirements of ex-internationals who are now club players, and do not word them as if current national-team news; prefer selection bombshells such as a surprise recall or a new captain. _(added 2026-11-27)_
- Name the series or match accurately: do not write 'in New Zealand series' for a story about the four-Test South Africa–New Zealand rivalry series. _(added 2026-11-27)_
- Prefer players or coaches leaving, suspended or injured (Sheehan ban, Sititi to Stade Français) over routine captaincy or lineup rotations. _(added 2026-11-27)_
- Don't spend a slot on an obituary unless the person is a global name; use it only if a fan of another team would text it. _(added 2026-11-27)_
