# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Ensure roundup lines focus on the subject nation's team news rather than attributing an opponent's injury update to the briefing's team. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Check word counts; ensure all lines remain strictly under 12 words to maintain the headline style. _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Ensure the roundup items are written as punchy headlines (e.g., 'Darcy Graham ruled out for season') rather than news sentences (e.g., 'Ciaran Frawley joins Connacht'). _(added 2026-09-26)_
- Maintain the men's-by-default rule strictly: ensure all women's or U20 items carry their labels in the roundup as they do in the main body. _(added 2026-09-26)_
