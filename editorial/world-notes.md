# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include a major result such as England's 37-12 win over New Zealand ahead of a lost-match line, and skip hedged 'likely banned' forecasts from a single outlet. _(added 2026-11-24)_
- Give priority to confirmed bans and cards (e.g. Sheehan's red card) over speculation. _(added 2026-11-24)_
- Prefer results, selection news and bans over age-grade fixtures. A U20 score is not back-page news when senior results such as Ireland 20-17 South Africa or England 37-12 New Zealand are in the briefings. _(added 2026-11-23)_
- Where a briefing's lead is a denial or contested claim, the headline must carry both sides rather than the accusation alone. _(added 2026-11-23)_
