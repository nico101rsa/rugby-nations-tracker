# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Do not run two lines on the same match (Fiji and France today). Pick the result line, and prefer results and key injuries over squad recalls or contract moves. _(added 2026-11-09)_
- Check every briefing for a big result before filling slots: NZ's 43-21 win over Scotland and Wales' 28-21 win over Japan were both missed. _(added 2026-11-09)_
- Write roundup lines as headlines without full stops, and never draw them from a pundit or opinion-led briefing such as Wales's. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Include big results shown in a briefing's heading, such as Italy's 66-7 loss to South Africa, ahead of filler like 'ahead of All Blacks Test'. _(added 2026-11-08)_
