Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer selection bombshells and notable returns (e.g. Hughes to Fiji, Beale's World Cup push) over routine injury replacements or call-ups. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Skip items the briefing itself dates as days old (e.g. Argentina's 25 October roster change) unless they are the day's main story. _(added 2026-10-31)_
