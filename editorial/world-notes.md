# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer genuine selection bombshells and key injuries (e.g. veterans dropped, a fly-half's fitness) over minor training returns or omissions framed by a pundit. _(added 2026-11-02)_
- Do not use 'first' or similar superlatives unless the briefing says so; cut each line strictly from what the briefing states, as a headline rather than a full sentence. _(added 2026-11-02)_
- Exclude court and legal cases and second-string or 'A' team results from the roundup; use Tests, key injuries, selection bombshells and coaching changes. _(added 2026-11-02)_
- Prefer an injury or selection story that affects a Test, such as Prendergast's fitness or Italy's squad, over minor fixtures. Avoid hype verbs like 'thrash'. _(added 2026-11-02)_
