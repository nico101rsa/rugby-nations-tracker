# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Never label a club player as a national age-grade player (e.g. 'France U18'); state club and age group accurately, and skip girls' or women's club stories in the men's roundup. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Only cut a line from the nation's own briefing, and prefer Test-level results and injuries (NZ injury list, Fiji's win over Japan) over club-match scares. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Only use a line when the nation's own team is affected. Do not frame a club move (Kolbe to the Stormers) as a loss for the national team. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Prioritise Test results and coach hires (such as Fiji's win in White's first game, or Edwards at Argentina) over minor squad swaps, and do not imply a training-only call-up is a selection. _(added 2026-10-25)_
