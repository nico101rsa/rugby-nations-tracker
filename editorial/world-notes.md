# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip stories that have already run in earlier editions (e.g. Edwards/Ledesma joining Argentina) and take lines from the lead item, not a closing body sentence. _(added 2026-10-16)_
- Prefer selection news for a big fixture (e.g. NZ's eight changes for the second Bledisloe) over a minor-injury line when both are available. _(added 2026-10-16)_
- Treat a new head coach being unveiled or named as a headline-worthy roundup item, and check the gate's women's/age-grade flag against the briefing's actual content (Fiji's was a men's piece). _(added 2026-10-15)_
- Skip results more than a week old, and women's fixtures, unless the result is major, since another team's fan would not text a mate about them. _(added 2026-10-15)_
