# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include a line only if the briefing body itself states the event, not just its heading (e.g. the NZ 'drop Lomax' claim). _(added 2026-11-05)_
- Keep 'reportedly' or 'set to' on selections that come from a media report rather than a team announcement, and skip routine picks. _(added 2026-11-05)_
- Prefer a key injury or selection shock (e.g. a captain ruled out) over routine squad returns; swap the Ireland returns line for Italy's absences if it is stronger. _(added 2026-11-05)_
- Skip second-string or A-team call-ups (e.g. Argentina XV vs England A); prefer results, bombshell selections or key injuries to the senior side. _(added 2026-11-04)_
