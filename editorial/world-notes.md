# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer fresh results, selection bombshells and key injuries; skip results from days earlier and routine 'set to be fit' team news. _(added 2026-11-11)_
- Do not add colour such as 'maul-heavy' that is not in the briefing's own factual claim; keep each line a plain headline. _(added 2026-11-11)_
- Include at least one big result (e.g. a heavy Nations Championship win or loss) when the briefings carry one, rather than filling all four slots with injuries. _(added 2026-11-11)_
- Prefer results, selection bombshells and coaching changes over single-player injuries; use no more than two injury lines. _(added 2026-11-11)_
