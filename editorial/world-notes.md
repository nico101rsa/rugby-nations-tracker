# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Report only what the briefing states: if it says 'to have surgery', do not write 'has surgery'. _(added 2026-11-29)_
- Prefer completed results (Ireland 38-14 Australia, Italy 23-21 Fiji) over minor selection changes, and fill all four slots when the briefings hold qualifying stories. _(added 2026-11-29)_
- Prefer results and selection bombshells from the day's biggest matches, such as Italy v Fiji and Ireland v Australia, and key lineup changes such as South Africa's fly-half switch, over minor milestones like a missed 100th cap or a routine HIA exit. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- Check that each line's claim sits in the source briefing, and skip a team's story when its lead is a minor non-selection or a single in-game knock. _(added 2026-11-28)_
