# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer results (scorelines), key injuries and coaching or selection news. Leave out bids, administrative items and speculation. Include a reported Test scoreline when any briefing carries one. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Don't compress a briefing's claim into something stronger or different (e.g. 'start' for a 'restart'). Check timing words against the briefing text. _(added 2026-10-17)_
- Give priority to coach appointments and selection bombshells (e.g. Edwards joining Argentina) over minor squad-naming items when choosing the four lines. _(added 2026-10-16)_
- Skip stories that have already run in earlier editions (e.g. Edwards/Ledesma joining Argentina) and take lines from the lead item, not a closing body sentence. _(added 2026-10-16)_
