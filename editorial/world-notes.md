# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Copy descriptors such as a player's position exactly from the briefing. 'Ex-England back' for Hughes is wrong or ambiguous, so use 'ex-England' alone if unsure. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Do not run a line about a story that the destination nation's own briefing omits without it reading as complete. For Edwards, state the hire plainly: 'Shaun Edwards joins Argentina's coaching staff'. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Include only items a rival fan would text about. Skip 'cleared of injury' non-events like Hooper, and prefer real availability news such as Italy's 17 unavailable players. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Write headlines, not sentences: no trailing full stop, and drop the repeated 'Nation's Player' formula where a plain verb headline works. _(added 2026-10-31)_
