# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Do not cut roundup lines from opinion-column briefings, and do not recycle an old ban or sacking as news; use only stories reported fresh in that edition. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- Skip workload, fatigue or rest absences unless it is a genuine injury or selection bombshell; prefer results, bans and major team news. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- Skip bench-level or squad-depth injuries (e.g. a replacement prop); keep only starters, captains, coaches or results that another team's fans would text about. _(added 2026-11-21)_
- Do not include a previous week's result as if it were today's news unless the briefing itself frames it as fresh; prefer a same-day event. _(added 2026-11-21)_
