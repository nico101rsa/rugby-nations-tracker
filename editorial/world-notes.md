# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Use the briefing's own verb. Write 'call up' or 'name', not 'recall', unless the player has previously played for that nation. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Skip routine squad additions, replacement call-ups and A-team results. Prefer Test results, major injuries, coaching changes and genuine selection bombshells. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Skip items the briefing itself dates as old (e.g. 'back in January') and routine club results or signings; roundup lines need a fresh, nation-level story. _(added 2026-10-31)_
- Prefer international-level news (squad bombshells, key injuries, rulings) over opinion-derived or club-level facts when choosing the four lines. _(added 2026-10-31)_
