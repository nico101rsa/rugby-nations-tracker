# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Where a line rests on one person's account (e.g. 'Edwards says Borthwick turned down offer'), keep the attribution in the headline or skip it. _(added 2026-11-03)_
- Skip routine squad-list omissions (uncapped players left out of a 23); prefer injuries, selection surprises and results. _(added 2026-11-03)_
- Cut each roundup line from the lead event of that nation's briefing, not from background detail in its body (e.g. Beale's old selection in the Australia piece). _(added 2026-11-03)_
- Prefer a fresh team announcement or union-level bombshell (e.g. South Africa's Italy 23, the URC legal threat to the WRU) over a stale squad item when choosing the four lines. _(added 2026-11-03)_
