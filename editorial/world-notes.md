# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include the biggest men's Test results (e.g. SA's 66-7 win over Italy) ahead of A-team or non-Test fixtures like England A v Argentina XV. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Don't write 'open Nations Championship' unless the source briefing says it is the opener of the competition; use 'beat Scotland 43-21' only. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Prefer results and key injuries (e.g. Ireland's 42-12 win, Sheehan's failed HIA) over cap milestones like McReight's 50th. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Attribute contested newspaper allegations in a roundup headline (e.g. 'Times claims...'), and prefer a result or selection line over unverified reports when both exist. _(added 2026-11-07)_
