# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Use only today's events from each briefing's lead; skip prior-week results that appear as background context (e.g. 'Wales beat Japan 28-21'). _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Include a major fixture result, such as France v South Africa, whenever any briefing carries it. Don't cut four routine lines when a big result is in the pack. _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Always include a major result, such as France beating South Africa 21-19, ahead of routine injuries or one-match captaincy lines. _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Prefer decided results and selection bombshells over single-match absences; keep each line a cut-down headline of 12 words or fewer. _(added 2026-11-14)_
