# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include the weekend's biggest results (e.g. Wales beating New Zealand, France beating South Africa) in the four lines ahead of minor selection news such as a single player's recall. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Where a briefing hedges ('understands', 'expected'), keep the hedge in the roundup headline instead of stating it as fact. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Include big men's results from any briefing, such as France 21-19 South Africa or Argentina 19-14 Italy. Don't publish just one line when several qualify. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Cut each line only from the nation's own briefing, with the numbers that briefing carries. Don't pull week-old results (England 45-22 Australia) into the roundup from another nation's story. _(added 2026-11-15)_
