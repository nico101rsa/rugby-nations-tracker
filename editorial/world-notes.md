# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip ranking movements and other non-events. Prefer selection bombshells, coach debuts and key injuries from other briefings, such as a first squad under a new head coach or a notable uncapped call-up. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Use as many of the four slots as the briefings justify. Two lines when Fiji and Australia selection stories were available is thin. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Do not add movement words ('drop', 'rise', 'surge') unless the briefing states a change; write 'Wallabies seventh after back-to-back Bledisloe defeats'. _(added 2026-10-23)_
- Skip lines cut from briefings built on opinion pieces, and prefer hard men's news (squads, results, appointments), including ones from the Fiji–Japan Test, when available. _(added 2026-10-23)_
