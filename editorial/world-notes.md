# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip minor squad-rotation or captaincy items and single-player knocks; prefer results, bombshells and coaching changes. Only state an injury duration if the briefing says the player is currently out for that long. _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Count a major selection bombshell (for example Dupont at fly-half for France) as roundup material even when it surfaces only in another nation's briefing, and prefer it over routine team-sheet tweaks. _(added 2026-11-14)_
- Don't use sin-bins or minor squad swaps as headlines. Prefer selection bombshells, captaincy changes and key injuries, and don't repeat a result that has not changed. _(added 2026-11-13)_
- Don't write 'sent off' for a temporary suspension. Use 'sin-binned' or 'yellow card' so the roundup does not overstate it. _(added 2026-11-13)_
