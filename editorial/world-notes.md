# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Skip club transfers of players outside a national squad; prefer results, selection calls, key Test-player injuries and coaching changes, such as a growing All Blacks injury list. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Fill up to four lines when the briefings carry qualifying stories rather than stopping at two low-salience items. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Prioritise Test results and captain- or star-level selection and injury news (e.g. Fiji's win over Japan, Savea unavailable) over club-match injuries with no severity reported. _(added 2026-10-25)_
- Do not imply a cause or sequence the briefing lacks: Faletau was left out of the squad before his finger surgery, not because of it. _(added 2026-10-25)_
