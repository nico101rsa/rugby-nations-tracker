Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Cut each headline from a briefing that actually states the score. Wales 35-28 All Blacks is in the New Zealand briefing and France 21-19 South Africa is in the South Africa briefing, so use those rather than letting senior men's results drop. _(added 2026-11-16)_
- Rank senior men's Test results above age-grade results when only four slots exist; use an age-grade line only if no major Test result is missing. _(added 2026-11-16)_
- Always include a major result, such as a historic upset or a big-margin win, when a briefing carries it. Wales's first win over New Zealand since 1953 should have made today's cut ahead of a selection note. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Do not repeat a briefing heading's blame framing (e.g. 'after Garbisi's missed touch-kick'). State the result neutrally, e.g. 'Argentina beat Italy 19-14 in Genoa'. _(added 2026-11-15)_
