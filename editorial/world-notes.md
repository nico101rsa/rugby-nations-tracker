Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Cut each line only from facts stated in that nation's own briefing; do not import the reason or detail for a ban or injury from another team's edition. _(added 2026-11-21)_
- Skip routine selections such as a player returning to the XV; prefer bans, injuries, coach pressure and results other fans would text about. _(added 2026-11-21)_
