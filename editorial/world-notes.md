# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer national-team headlines (captain ruled out, results, debuts such as Savea's absence or White's Fiji win) over club-match injuries or players already left out of squads. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Cut roundup lines from the briefing's lead news only; do not lift old results mentioned as context (e.g. Scotland's Round 3 win). _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Check the direction of every result line (winner and loser) against the briefing. Japan's line reversed Fiji's 34-28 win. Tag each line only with a nation whose own briefing contains it. _(added 2026-10-24)_
- Prefer results, key injuries and genuine selection bombshells. Skip routine squad lines such as uncapped call-ups and debutants unless the player is a star. _(added 2026-10-24)_
