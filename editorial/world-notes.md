# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Attribute contested newspaper allegations in a roundup headline (e.g. 'Times claims...'), and prefer a result or selection line over unverified reports when both exist. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Include a headline-making result such as a big Test win when one is in the briefings (e.g. Ireland 42-12 Argentina); rank it above routine selection items. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Prefer key injuries (e.g. a player out for 12 weeks) and genuine selection shocks over a routine uncapped starter. _(added 2026-11-07)_
- Only include a line for an event that broke in today's edition; do not recycle background facts such as Jake White's months-old Fiji appointment as news. _(added 2026-11-07)_
