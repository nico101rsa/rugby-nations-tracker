# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Only use results or tables that are new today; drop restated old results such as Japan's loss to Fiji when the briefing is a schedule note. _(added 2026-10-30)_
- Skip routine uncapped call-ups and minor squad news; with fewer than four genuine talking points, publish fewer lines. _(added 2026-10-30)_
- Prefer key injuries and selection news (e.g. a nation's 17 unavailable players or three France withdrawals) over individual awards-night items when choosing the four lines. _(added 2026-10-30)_
- Include results and selection bombshells from other editions (e.g. Fiji's 34-28 win over Japan, Beale's Wallabies recall) ahead of lesser items. _(added 2026-10-29)_
