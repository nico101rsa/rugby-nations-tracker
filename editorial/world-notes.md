# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Only include confirmed injuries or selections. Drop tentative 'concern' items such as Tuipulotu, and prefer firmer national-team stories such as the Erasmus/Furlong ruling when they are in a briefing. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Prioritise key injuries and squad bombshells (e.g. Springboks missing Kolbe, Etzebeth, Pollard; NZ's Lomax) over governance or business speculation. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Write single-source reports as reported ('reportedly', 'WalesOnline says') rather than flat fact, and keep lines faithful to the briefing's own wording. _(added 2026-11-01)_
- Use the briefing's own verb. Write 'call up' or 'name', not 'recall', unless the player has previously played for that nation. _(added 2026-11-01)_
