Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include big men's results from any briefing, such as France 21-19 South Africa or Argentina 19-14 Italy. Don't publish just one line when several qualify. _(added 2026-11-15)_
- Cut each line only from the nation's own briefing, with the numbers that briefing carries. Don't pull week-old results (England 45-22 Australia) into the roundup from another nation's story. _(added 2026-11-15)_
