# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- When a briefing leads with a match result (e.g. South Africa's 20-17 loss to Ireland), cut the roundup line from that result rather than a side item like a hearing request. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- Prefer headline results (e.g. England 37-12 NZ, Scotland 67-5 Japan) and key injuries over milestones; skip anything cut from an opinion-led briefing. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- Flag second-string or development sides (e.g. 'Fiji's second-string Latui') so readers don't mistake them for the senior national team. _(added 2026-11-22)_
- Prefer results, bans and sackings over team-news lines for matches already played; Montoya returning for a completed France test is stale. _(added 2026-11-22)_
