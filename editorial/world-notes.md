# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prioritise men's national-side results and selection news (e.g. the 43-21 Bledisloe result) over routine club fixtures; never fill three of four slots with club items. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Include a major Tier 1 men's Test result or coach appointment when one exists in the briefings, rather than a club result with no national consequence. _(added 2026-10-19)_
- Do not run two lines on the same match or its fallout (e.g. a result plus the rankings change it caused); keep the result and spend the other slot on a different story. _(added 2026-10-18)_
- Prefer results, selection bombshells, key injuries and coaching changes over administrative items such as hosting bids; leave a slot empty rather than fill it with bid news. _(added 2026-10-18)_
