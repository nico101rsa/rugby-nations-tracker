# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Write back-page headlines as fragments without terminal full stops (e.g. 'Fiji beat Japan 34-28 in centenary Test'). _(added 2026-10-27)_
- Prefer fresh NEW selection, discipline and injury stories over older background match reports, and only include a result if it is current. _(added 2026-10-27)_
- Vary the four lines across story types: prefer a disciplinary case, squad reshuffle or selection bombshell over stacking several injuries. _(added 2026-10-27)_
- Keep source hedges ('set to', 'likely', 'faces a scan') in the headline and never upgrade them to certainty; do not cut a line from an opinion column. _(added 2026-10-27)_
