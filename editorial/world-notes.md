# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Do not drop nuance from the edition. If the edition says first time at a venue, do not headline it as simply 'first time'. Say where an injury happened if the briefing does. _(added 2026-11-13)_
- Prefer fresh selection bombshells (e.g. England's 10 changes) over days-old disciplinary items like a yellow card. _(added 2026-11-13)_
- Skip hedged selection tips ('poised', 'set to') like Wales's Basham line. Prefer confirmed results, injuries to key players, and coach appointments or sackings. _(added 2026-11-12)_
- Prefer fresh results, selection bombshells and key injuries; skip results from days earlier and routine 'set to be fit' team news. _(added 2026-11-11)_
