# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Leave out routine selections and picks drawn from feature pieces (e.g. a fly-half starting). Keep results, bans, injuries and coaching changes. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- When several nations' briefings report the same match, use one line for it and fill the other slots with different stories. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- Prefer selection bombshells and fresh results (e.g. a team's 11 changes) over stale injuries from previous weeks. _(added 2026-11-28)_
- Do not carry a result line unless the briefing's result is consistent with the other team's briefing. _(added 2026-11-28)_
