# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Include the day's major results (e.g. South Africa 66-7 Italy, Ireland 42-12 Argentina, Wales 28-21 Japan) ahead of labour or administrative stories; fill up to four lines when the briefings support it. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Use plain headline wording taken from the briefing's own heading (say "union" or "players", not unexplained jargon like "Rupa"), and only run pay or contract disputes if they affect a Test. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Include every major men's result from the day's matches (e.g. NZ v Scotland, Ireland v Argentina) before hedged 'reports say' selection items. _(added 2026-11-08)_
- Drop lines that need 'reports say' or leave the key person unnamed. Prefer a confirmed result, injury or team announcement. _(added 2026-11-08)_
