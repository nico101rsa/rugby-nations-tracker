# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Prefer results and coach sackings or reviews over routine team news: Ireland's 38-14 win over Australia and Japan's Eddie Jones review outrank Scotland's captaincy line. _(added 2026-11-29)_
- Don't spend a slot on a captain's return or a selection that is merely routine; keep slots for results, injuries to key players, and coaching changes. _(added 2026-11-29)_
- Do not cut roundup lines from opinion columns, previews of matches already played, or club coaching appointments. Prefer final results such as Italy 23-21 Fiji and Ireland 38-14 Australia, and key injuries such as Menoncello's surgery. _(added 2026-11-29)_
- Skip stories more than a few days old, such as the 21 Nov Vivas red card, when a result or selection from the current weekend is available. _(added 2026-11-29)_
