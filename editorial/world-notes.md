# Standing roundup notes

Injected into the "Around the world" prompt daily; curated by the post-run review.
Notes age out after 14 days unless re-earned. Prune freely.

- Only use growth or escalation words ('grows', 'mounts') if the briefing reports new injuries or developments; a list of existing injuries is not 'growing'. _(added 2026-11-19)_
- Prefer headline results and selection bombshells, such as a historic win over the All Blacks, over secondary injury lists when the briefing carries them. _(added 2026-11-19)_
- Prioritise coach bans or sackings and landmark upsets (e.g. Erasmus banned, Wales beat All Blacks) over scheduling news or week-old results. _(added 2026-11-18)_
- If a briefing is an opinion piece but its body states a hard news fact such as a ban, cut the headline from that fact, not the opinion. _(added 2026-11-18)_
